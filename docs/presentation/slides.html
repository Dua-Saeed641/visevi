Problem statement and target audience

A warning is not proof, and proof rarely reaches the right person in time.

2 / 6

The problem

  • Signals and evidence are disconnected. A forecast or sensor produces a number, but nobody can quickly see, verify and document what is happening on the ground.
  • Field evidence is manual and easy to fake. Photos are sorted by hand, reused across reports, and hard to trace to their source.
  • Warning coverage is incomplete. Half of the world's countries still lack multi-hazard early warning systems; the UN goal is coverage for everyone by 2027.
  • Even impact figures are contested. Press reports of India's national surveillance data give 56 confirmed heatstroke deaths for March to May 2024 and 14 for March to June 2024.

Who faces it

B2G: primary

State and district disaster management authorities, municipal corporations, control-room officers. Typically professionals in their late 20s to 50s.

B2B

Parametric and crop insurers verifying events, utilities and infrastructure operators, agri-cooperatives.

NGOs and sustainability teams

Humanitarian programme teams, CSR and ESG auditors who must prove what happened, where and when.

Indirect beneficiaries

Communities in heat-, flood- and cyclone-prone regions who get a faster, better-founded warning. No B2C product at this stage.

Our unique solution

Signal, satellite proof, verdict, notification: one closed loop.

3 / 6
1

Signal watchLive temperature and coastal waves at every monitored site, judged against ten years of local history plus fixed limits. Field sensors post to a webhook.

2

Automatic satellite captureAn alert pulls a fresh NASA Worldview snapshot of the site, screens out cloud and no-data days, and sends it through Cloudinary (enhance, AI Vision).

3

Before / after verdictCompared with the same season a year earlier; vegetation, water-like area and cloud are measured. Result: confirmed, not confirmed or inconclusive.

4

Notify the right personResponsible contacts get email or free ntfy push, with the evidence and a full audit log of every message.

5

Risk index with confidence0 to 100 from six sourced factors (NASA hazard maps, live, forecast, incidents, nearby events, imagery), plus a separate confidence score.

6

Trust layerSatellite images are re-requested from NASA to prove provenance; duplicates, wrong content and false dates are flagged; every asset traces to its Cloudinary ID and transformations.

Novelty

  • Sensor-triggered, satellite-verified alerts in one product.
  • Honest by design: "inconclusive" is a real answer; a metric that could not be calibrated was dropped, not shown.
  • Cloudinary beyond storage: pre-processing, AI Vision, provenance, and a one-URL before/after board with the reading stamped on it.

Feasibility, showstoppers, USP and business model

Built, running, and honest about its limits.

5 / 6

Feasibility: it already runs

  • Working prototype: 19 monitored sites, 117 captures across 23 projects (95% verified), 22 alerts, including a replayed May 2024 heat wave (Barmer 48.0 °C).
  • Zero paid data: every source is free and keyless; only Cloudinary and MongoDB accounts are needed.
  • Tested end to end with real NASA and Cloudinary calls. The 15-minute poll raised and captured a live alert without anyone pressing a button.
  • Team fit: full-stack TypeScript, geospatial data, Cloudinary; built in incremental milestones inside the hackathon window.

Showstoppers and mitigation

RiskMitigation
Satellite resolution is about 250 m per pixel"Inconclusive" is a first-class verdict; roadmap: Sentinel-2 at 10 m
Cloud cover and no-data daysAutomatic screening, retry over 10 days, reuse a recent capture
Free-API terms and limitsCaching, provider swap (Google Weather optional), paid plans when commercial
Cloudinary quotaCapture only when an alert fires; reuse within 8 days
False alarms, unvalidated weightsAnomaly must also be stressful; confidence score; back-test against EM-DAT next

USP

One product closes the loop from a live signal to a satellite-verified, notified alert, with a transparent risk score and a confidence value that says how far to trust it.

  • Confirms or questions every alert with imagery.
  • Provenance-checked evidence, not just organised evidence.
  • Free public data keeps the cost of each site low.

Business model

CustomersState and district disaster authorities (B2G); insurers and utilities (B2B); NGOs and CSR or ESG auditors
SubscriptionPer monitored site per month, tiered by site count and check frequency
Verified-event APIPer event or claim check for insurers, via webhook
ServicesOnboarding: thresholds, contacts and local-language setup
Free tierNGO and community use, built on open data
SustainabilityCosts are event-driven (one AI analysis per capture, only on alerts), so margin grows with sites, not with time
